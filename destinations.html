Goa, India

Attractions: Calangute Beach, Fort Aguada, Dudhsagar Falls, Basilica of Bom Jesus

Local Food: Fish Curry, Prawns, Bebinca, Feni, Seafood Thali

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Visit during monsoon for waterfalls. Respect local customs at churches. Best time: November to May.

Kerala, India

Attractions: Backwaters of Alleppey, Munnar Tea Gardens, Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, Kovalam Beach

Local Food: Appam with Stew, Puttu, Avial, Fish Curry, Sadhya

Budget Category: Medium

Travel Tips: Take a houseboat cruise in backwaters. Visit during Onam festival. Best time: September to March.

Rajasthan, India

Attractions: Amber Fort, City Palace Jaipur, Jaisalmer Fort, Udaipur City Palace

Local Food: Dal Baati Churma, Laal Maas, Gatte ki Sabzi, Ker Sangri, Rajasthani Thali

Budget Category: Medium

Travel Tips: Stay in heritage hotels. Visit during desert festivals. Best time: October to March.

Himachal Pradesh, India

Attractions: Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, Rohtang Pass, Solang Valley

Local Food: Dham, Chana Madra, Siddu, Patande, Apple Cider

Budget Category: Medium

Travel Tips: Carry warm clothes. Get inner line permits for restricted areas. Best time: March to June, September to November.

Uttarakhand, India

Attractions: Rishikesh, Haridwar, Nainital, Mussoorie, Valley of Flowers

Local Food: Aloo Ke Gutke, Kafuli, Jhangora, Bal Mithai, Garhwali Cuisine

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Visit during Char Dham Yatra. Stay in ashrams for spiritual experience. Best time: March to June.

Jammu & Kashmir, India

Attractions: Dal Lake, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Srinagar Mughal Gardens, Sonamarg

Local Food: Roganjosh, Yakhni, Dum Aloo, Kashmiri Pulao, Kahwa Tea

Budget Category: Medium

Travel Tips: Get proper permits. Respect local culture. Best time: April to October.

Delhi, India

Attractions: Red Fort, India Gate, Qutub Minar, Lotus Temple, Chandni Chowk

Local Food: Butter Chicken, Chole Bhature, Paratha, Kulfi, Street Food

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Use metro for transportation. Visit during Republic Day parade. Best time: October to March.

Mumbai, India

Attractions: Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Elephanta Caves, Bollywood Studios

Local Food: Vada Pav, Pav Bhaji, Bhel Puri, Misal Pav, Seafood

Budget Category: Medium

Travel Tips: Use local trains. Visit during monsoon for different experience. Best time: November to May.

Bangalore, India

Attractions: Cubbon Park, Bangalore Palace, ISKCON Temple, Lalbagh Botanical Garden

Local Food: Masala Dosa, Bisi Bele Bath, Filter Coffee, Ragi Mudde, Biryani

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Explore tech parks. Visit during Dasara festival. Best time: October to March.

Chennai, India

Attractions: Marina Beach, Kapaleeshwarar Temple, Fort St. George, San Thome Basilica

Local Food: Idli Sambar, Dosa, Chettinad Cuisine, Filter Coffee, Pongal

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Visit during Pongal festival. Explore colonial architecture. Best time: December to March.

Kolkata, India

Attractions: Victoria Memorial, Howrah Bridge, Kalighat Temple, South City Mall

Local Food: Rosogolla, Shorshe Ilish, Phuchka, Biryani, Sandesh

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Visit during Durga Puja. Take tram rides. Best time: October to March.

Agra, India

Attractions: Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri, Itmad-ud-Daulah

Local Food: Petha, Dal Moth, Bedai, Paratha, Kebabs

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Visit at sunrise/sunset. Buy marble crafts. Best time: October to March.

Jaipur, India

Attractions: Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Jal Mahal

Local Food: Dal Baati Churma, Laal Maas, Gatte ki Sabzi, Rajasthani Sweets

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Stay in havelis. Visit during elephant festival. Best time: October to March.

Varanasi, India

Attractions: Ganges Ghats, Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Sarnath, Ramnagar Fort

Local Food: Kachori, Banarasi Paan, Rabri, Lassi, Street Food

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Take boat ride on Ganges. Visit during festivals. Best time: October to March.

Amritsar, India

Attractions: Golden Temple, Jallianwala Bagh, Wagah Border, Partition Museum

Local Food: Sarson da Saag, Makki di Roti, Kulcha, Lassi, Chole Bhature

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Visit Golden Temple early morning. Experience border ceremony. Best time: October to March.

Local Food: Mysore Pak, Kosambari, Bisi Bele Bath, Maddur Vada, Filter Coffee

Budget Category: Low

Travel Tips: Visit during Dasara festival. Explore sandalwood factories. Best time: October to March.

Local Food: Hyderabadi Biryani, Haleem, Irani Chai, Double Ka Meetha, Kebabs
