💡 Budget Planning Tips

Book in Advance

Book hotels and trains 2-3 weeks ahead to get better rates and discounts.

Travel Off-Season

Visit during shoulder seasons to save on accommodation and enjoy fewer crowds.

Explore Local Food

Eat at local restaurants and street food stalls for authentic and budget-friendly meals.

Use Public Transport

Use buses, trains, and autos instead of taxis to save significantly on transport costs.

Group Discounts

Travel with a group to avail discounts on hotels, activities, and entry fees.

Plan Activities

Research free attractions and budget activities to maximize your travel experience.
